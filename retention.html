1. Purpose

This Data Retention Policy ("Policy") describes how China Telecom AI Technology Co., Ltd. ("XingChen-AGI", "we", "us") retains, stores, and deletes information collected and processed through the XingChen-AGI API services (the "Service").

2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • Content you submit to the Service ("Input");
  • Content generated by the Service ("Output");
  • Technical and usage logs (request timestamps, token volumes, endpoint calls);
  • Account and billing information.

3. Core Principle: No Default Retention of Prompts and Outputs

We design the Service so that, by default, your prompts and model outputs are not retained beyond what is necessary to deliver your request and return the response to you. This means:

  • Prompts are processed to generate your response and are not stored for any longer than required for that delivery.
  • Model outputs are returned to you and are not stored by us beyond the request lifecycle, except as described in Section 5 below.

This "no-default-retention" approach is intended to minimize the data we hold about your prompts and outputs.

4. Exception to Default Retention

The default no-retention policy does not apply to data that we are:

  • Legally required to retain under applicable law, regulation, or valid legal order (for example, data needed for regulatory or legal compliance);
  • Reasonably necessary to retain to prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents, or to investigate violations of our Terms of Service;
  • Retained with your explicit consent for a specific purpose.

Where such retention applies, we keep only the minimum amount of data needed for the specific lawful purpose, and we apply the retention periods in Section 6.

5. Logs, Usage and Billing Data

  • Technical and usage logs (request timestamps, endpoint, token usage, error codes) are retained for the minimum period necessary to operate, secure, and bill the Service — generally up to 12 months unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Billing and payment records are retained for the period required by tax and accounting law.
  • We do not link logs to your prompt content beyond what is needed for these operational purposes.

7. Deletion

When data reaches the end of its retention period, it is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymized. You may request deletion of your account and associated data; such requests are processed in accordance with applicable law and this Policy.

8. Data Access

Access to any data that is retained (for example, for a lawful purpose) is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, and is governed by our confidentiality and security controls.

9. Data Location, International Transfers and Third-Party Processors

Processing location. All data described in Section 2 is processed on servers located in mainland China. Input submitted to the Service is transmitted to mainland China for the purpose of generating Output, and Output is returned to you from there. Retention periods in Section 6 run from the time of receipt in mainland China.

Third-party processors. Where data is transferred to service providers or processors, we require them to comply with data protection requirements consistent with this Policy and applicable law. We do not transfer Input or Output to third parties for their own purposes.
