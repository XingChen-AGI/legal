## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how **China Telecom AI Technology Co., Ltd.** ("XingChen-AGI", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the XingChen-AGI API services (the "Service"), including the Xing model family.

### 1.1 Where your data is processed — Key Notice

**Input you send to the Service is transmitted to and processed on servers located in mainland China, and model Output is generated there.** This applies to every request, including requests that reach us through a third-party client, coding agent, gateway, routing service or model marketplace.

If you do not want your Input transferred to and processed in mainland China, do not use the Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

**2.1 Information You Provide**
- Account and registration information (e.g., name, email, billing details) when you create an account.
- API keys and authentication credentials.
- Content you submit to the Service ("Input"), including prompts, text, and any data you transmit through the APIs.
- Payment information, processed by our payment processors.

**2.2 Information Collected Automatically**
- Log and usage data, such as request timestamps, endpoints accessed, token usage volumes, and technical identifiers.
- Device and network information (IP address, browser or client type) for security and operational purposes.

**2.3 Model Output**
- We may process model-generated Output for the purpose of operating, securing, and improving the Service. Except as described in Section 4, Output is processed to fulfill your requests and maintain service quality.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
- Authenticate users and manage API keys;
- Monitor and enforce rate limits and prevent abuse or fraud;
- Improve model performance, service quality, and reliability;
- Comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights;
- Communicate with you about the Service, including billing and support.

## 4. Data Retention and Caching

- **Model Input and Output.** Consistent with our Data Retention Policy, we generally **do not store** your Input and Output beyond what is necessary to deliver the request, except where required for security, legal compliance, or where you have separately agreed. Please review our [Retention Policy](RETENTION.md).
- **Caching.** The Service uses automatic (implicit) caching to improve response speed and efficiency. Cached results are managed under the same data handling and retention rules set out in this policy.
- **Rate Limiting.** We may retain technical logs (such as timestamps and usage volumes) for rate-limiting and abuse-prevention purposes.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do **not sell** your personal information. We may share information only:
- With service providers and processors who support the Service, under confidentiality obligations;
- Where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process;
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Service, our users, or the public;
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you where required.

**6.3 PRC law.** Processing by us is also subject to the laws of the People's Republic of China, including the Personal Information Protection Law ("PIPL"). Where PRC law requires disclosure of information to a competent authority, we disclose only what is legally required, review each request for legal validity and scope, and — where lawful and technically feasible — notify the party that submitted the request or the affected account holder.
